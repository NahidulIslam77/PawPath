# 🐾 PawPath

### A Smart Pet Adoption & Foster Management Platform

PawPath is a web-based pet adoption and fostering platform designed to connect people with pets in need of loving homes. The platform simplifies the adoption and fostering process by helping users discover available pets, evaluate their eligibility, submit applications, and track application status.

PawPath also provides dedicated management systems for shelter managers and administrators, allowing them to manage pets, applications, users, shelters, and platform activities efficiently.

## 📌 Project Overview

Finding a suitable pet to adopt or foster can be challenging for both potential pet owners and animal shelters. PawPath aims to make this process more organized, transparent, and accessible through a centralized digital platform.

Before applying for adoption or fostering, users can complete an **Eligibility Quiz** that evaluates basic requirements such as:

- Availability of time for daily pet care
- Ability to afford pet-related expenses
- Availability of a safe living environment

Based on the user's responses, the system determines whether the user meets the basic eligibility criteria for adoption or fostering.

Eligible users can then explore available pets, view detailed pet information, and submit adoption or foster applications.

### 🧠 Eligibility Quiz

PawPath includes an eligibility assessment system that evaluates whether a user meets basic requirements before applying for adoption or fostering.

The quiz evaluates factors such as:

- Daily availability for pet care
- Financial ability to support a pet
- Safety and suitability of the living environment

The system provides an eligibility result based on the user's answers.

> **Note:** The eligibility quiz is intended as a preliminary screening tool and does not replace the final approval process conducted by shelters or authorized personnel.

### 🏠 Pet Adoption

Users can explore pets available for adoption and view important information, including:

- Pet Name
- Age
- Gender
- Pet Type
- Activity Level
- Health Status
- Description
- Availability

Users who are eligible can submit an adoption application by providing information such as:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Home Address
- Living Situation
- Preferred Adoption Date
- Previous Adoption or Foster Experience
- Reason for Adoption

---

### 🐕 Pet Fostering

PawPath also supports temporary pet fostering.

Users can view available foster pets and information such as:

- Pet Name
- Age
- Gender
- Pet Type
- Activity Level
- Health Status
- Foster Duration
- Pet Description

Users can submit foster applications by providing:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Address
- Desired Foster Duration
- Living Situation
- Previous Foster Experience

---

### 📊 Application Tracking

Users can monitor the status of their submitted applications through their personal dashboard.

Application statuses may include:

- Pending
- Under Review
- Approved
- Rejected
- Completed

This provides users with a transparent way to track their adoption and foster requests.
